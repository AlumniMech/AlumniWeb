About the SoME-MITS-DU Alumni Network

How a vision in 2023 became a 1,065-member association — and what it's building next.

Our story

Established in 2023, the Alumni Mechanical Cell was founded with a clear vision: to create a lasting bridge between the institution and its graduates. What began as an initiative to stay connected has evolved into a structured platform fostering mentorship, industry exposure, and collaborative growth. Guided by this vision, the Cell continues to strengthen ties between alumni and current students, enriching the academic journey of the department as a whole. Introducing the Alumni Mechanical Cell The Alumni Mechanical Cell, School of Mechanical Engineering, was established in 2023 to strengthen the bond between our esteemed alumni and the institution. It serves as a platform for meaningful engagement, knowledge exchange, and collaborative growth between past and present members of our academic community. Our vision is to build a strong, lifelong network that fosters mentorship, industry-academia collaboration, and continuous professional development, ultimately contributing to the holistic advancement of both our students and the department.

Mission & objectives

Reconnect

Give alumni a voice in how the department evolves its curriculum and facilities.
